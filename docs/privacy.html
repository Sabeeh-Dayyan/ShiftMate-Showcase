Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information the ShiftMate app and website collect, why, and what you can do about it. ShiftMate is provided by Sabeeh Dayyan ("we", "us"). You can contact us about privacy at sabeehdayyanofficial@gmail.com.

Who is responsible for your information

ShiftMate is used by businesses ("employers") to run rotas, time clocks and team updates for their staff.

  • Your employer decides what work information is recorded about you in their workplace, such as your shifts, timesheets, leave and pay rates. For that information your employer is the controller and we process it on their behalf. Questions about it are best sent to your employer first.
  • We are the controller for your ShiftMate account itself (your login and profile), for the business owner's account and subscription, and for keeping the service secure.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider)To create your account and let you sign in
Phone number and job title (optional)So your team can reach you; you choose whether coworkers see your number
Workplace information: locations, jobs, shifts, swaps, availability, leave requests and balances, timesheets, tasks, posts, comments and documentsTo provide the service your employer uses
Precise location, only at the moment you clock in, start or end a break, or clock outSo your employer can see whether you were at work when you clocked in. ShiftMate never tracks your location in the background.
Photos you choose to take when clocking in, and photos or videos you add to postsTo show them to the people your employer allows
Pay rates and pay rules entered by your employerTo estimate labour costs. Only owners, admins and managers with pay permission can see them; team members never can.
A device token for notificationsTo send you alerts about your shifts and your team
Business details for owners: business name, country and VAT number (optional)To set up the workplace, its currency and its plan
Basic technical logs (for example, when a request failed)To keep the service working and secure

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to make automated decisions about your pay or job. ShiftMate does not use face recognition or any biometric matching.

Legal bases

We process account and service information to perform our contract with the business that uses ShiftMate, and to provide the service you signed up for. We keep the service secure and prevent misuse (for example, one free trial per business) because we have a legitimate interest in doing so. Location is collected only when you choose to clock in or out, and your phone asks for your permission first.

Who we share it with

  • People in your workplace, according to their role. For example, managers see the rota and timesheets for their locations; coworkers see published shifts and posts.
  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud), which hosts ShiftMate's database, files, sign-in and notifications for us. Our database and files are stored in London. Google may process some information in other countries under its data processing terms and standard contractual clauses.
  • A payment provider, once paid plans are available, to handle payments for business owners. We never store card details.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your login, profile, phone number, availability, clock-in photos and notifications are deleted straight away. Records your employer needs for payroll and legal reasons, such as past timesheets and pay history, stay with your employer and show you as "Deleted user".
  • When a workplace's plan ends, its information is kept for at least 30 days so the owner can renew, then deleted on request or when we close inactive workplaces.
  • When an owner deletes their workplace, everything in it is deleted.
  • To stop the free trial being claimed again and again, we keep a one-way scrambled code made from the owner's email and business name. It can't be turned back into either.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw any consent. Email sabeehdayyanofficial@gmail.com. For information your employer controls, we will pass your request to them or help them answer it. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. In the United Kingdom that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each workplace's data is kept separate, and access follows each person's role, enforced on our servers. Every view of pay information is logged.

Children

ShiftMate is for businesses and their staff. It is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

We will update this page when this policy changes and, for significant changes, tell business owners in the app or by email.
