Delete your ShiftMate account

You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time:

  1. Open ShiftMate and go to More → Profile and privacy.
  2. Tap Delete my account and enter your password to confirm.

If you can't sign in, email sabeehdayyanofficial@gmail.com from the address you use for ShiftMate with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, name, email address, phone number and profile
  • Your availability, clock-in photos and notifications
  • Your upcoming shifts are taken off you so your manager can fill them

What your employer keeps

Records your employer needs for payroll and legal reasons, such as past timesheets, past shifts and pay history, stay with the workplace and show you as "Deleted user".

Workplace owners

If you own a workplace and other people still use it, hand ownership to someone else first (Team → their profile). If you're the only member, deleting your account deletes the workplace and everything in it.
