Common questions

How do I add my team?

In the app, go to Team → Invite, create an invite code and share it. Staff download ShiftMate, sign up and enter the code.

Why does ShiftMate ask for my location?

Only to check you're at work when you clock in or out, if your employer has set a clock-in pin. It never tracks you in the background.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and we'll email you a reset link.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check notifications are allowed for ShiftMate in your phone's settings, and look at More → Notification settings for muted alerts or quiet hours.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Everyone can still sign in and see everything, but changes pause until the owner chooses a plan. Staff can still clock in and out for 7 days.
